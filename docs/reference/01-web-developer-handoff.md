organizations, and communities. Based in Utah, working virtually and on site

#### Accessibility rules for color Approved

  * **#C8902F on cream fails AA for small text.** Use **#855D16 (gold-700)** for any gold text under 18px, including eyebrows, links, captions, and small labels. Gold-500 is fine for large display type, icons, rules, and button fills.

## 05Spacing & layout

  * **Max content width** 1200px; inner text columns 640–760px. Long body copy never runs the full width.
  * **Section padding** 88px top and bottom on desktop, 56px on tablet, 44px on mobile. Hero sections 72–110px top.
  * **Horizontal page padding** 40px desktop, 24px mobile.
  * **Card padding** 24–40px; grid gaps 22px; chip and tag gaps 10px.
  * **Button spacing** 12–14px between buttons in a row; 44px minimum tap height everywhere.
  * **Corner radius** small 8px, medium 12px, large 20px. Cards and callouts use large; buttons use pill or 12px consistently, not both.
  * **Shadows** soft and diffuse only: `0 8px 22px rgba(27,33,39,0.10)`. Nothing hard-edged.
  * **Section rhythm** alternate cream, white, and one dark sage-to-ink band per page at most. Two background colors per page is the ceiling.

The site should feel spacious and calm. Avoid dense pages, card pile-ups,
decorative dividers that do no work, crowded icon grids, tiny text, and long
blocks of centered body copy — center short statements only.
