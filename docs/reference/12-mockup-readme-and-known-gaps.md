THREE PLACES THE MOCKUP PREDATES THE FINAL ARCHITECTURE
  The Web Developer Handoff controls in each case. Do not build these
  from the mockup:

  1. PRICING PAGE. The mockup contains a standalone Pricing view.
     There is NO standalone public Pricing page in the final
     architecture, and no Pricing item in the navigation. Those price
     cards belong in the Individual Sessions area of Services.

  2. PROVIDER PAGE FRAMING. The page is titled "For Therapists &
     Providers". Referral framing is retired: no formal referral is
     required or requested, there is no referral form, and there is no
     clinical intake pathway. Providers share information; individuals
     decide for themselves and contact Starting Point directly.

  3. INTEGRATION SUPPORT. No standalone page at launch - it is a
     section within Individual Sessions.

  Also note: the mockup shows the visual treatment of the checkout /
  agreement moment, but the final payment architecture is the seven
  Stripe Payment Links and the three private post-payment scheduling
  pages documented in the Web Developer Handoff, sections 04, 05 and 06.
