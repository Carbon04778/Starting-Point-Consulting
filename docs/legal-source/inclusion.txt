TITLE: Non-Discrimination & Inclusion Statement

INTRO: Starting Point Consulting is committed to creating learning environments where every person is treated with dignity, respect, and compassion.

---

We welcome individuals of all backgrounds, identities, abilities, ages, cultures, races, ethnicities, national origins, religions, genders, sexual orientations, veteran status, and lived experiences.

We believe that every person deserves to feel seen, heard, and valued. Our commitment is to provide inclusive, accessible, and trauma-aware educational experiences where people can learn, grow, and thrive together.

[CALLOUT] We do not tolerate discrimination, harassment, or exclusion in any of our programs, services, partnerships, or events.
